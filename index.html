Applies -3% each to TAS at each 15-minute step (only where TAS ≥ Vy), per RFM Section 10.0. Torque and fuel flow are unaffected (no correction given for those in the book).
Cruise Power Margin (FLI = 0.5 Below MCP)
Unchecked, cruise may plan right up to AEO Max Continuous. Checked: plan cruise 7% torque below AEO Max Continuous, IAW Flight Crew Operating Manual (FCOM). Costs a few knots where power is already near the limit — heavy, hot, or high.
Applies to the cruise steps only — the climb segment always targets 1% below AEO Max Continuous.
Departure Airfield
Used to size a single climb segment from field elevation up to cruise altitude — a simplified average-climb estimate, not a modeled climb profile. Inserted as the first row of the burn-down below, ahead of the 15-minute cruise steps.
TAS reduction during climb scales linearly with ROC: 10% off best-range TAS at 500 ft/min, up to 30% off at 1,500 ft/min. Fuel flow for the climb segment is looked up at the midpoint altitude/OAT between field elevation and cruise altitude, at departure weight.
